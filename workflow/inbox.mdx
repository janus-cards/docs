## Overview

The inbox is the new and default way to review your generated flashcards.

Once a document has been uploaded, and a request to generate flashcards has completed, the proposed deck for that document/[Notebook](/workflow/notebook/overview) will be grouped by highlight and shown in the inbox. You can then review and edit the cards, and when you're happy you can export. This moves the deck into the "Exported" folder to archive it.

Decks that are being processed are also displayed in the inbox, but with a loading spinner.
