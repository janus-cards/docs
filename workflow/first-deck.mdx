<Tip>
Welcome to **Janus**! This guide goes through everything you need to know to create your first deck in minutes.
</Tip>

## Step by Step Guide
<Steps>
<Step title="Upload"><p>
Select the material you want to make flashcards for, including:
- [PDFs](/workflow/upload/integrations/pdf) of your lecture notes, textbooks or research papers.
- [Text](/workflow/upload/integrations/text) from your digital note taking app.
- [YouTube](/workflow/upload/integrations/youtube) links of online lectures or tutorials.
- [URLs](/workflow/upload/integrations/url) of online essays, blogs and articles.
- [Audio](/workflow/upload/integrations/audio) of recorded lectures.
- [Readwise](/workflow/upload/integrations/readwise) highlights of books or podcasts.

![](/imgs/upload-example.png)
</p></Step>
<Step title="Generate"> <p>
Add a prompt for what you want highlighted and flashcards on. Then watch as Janus digests your material to create a deck. Save the cards you like and delete the ones you don't.
![](/imgs/generation-example-with-tabs.png)
</p></Step>
<Step title="Review Highlights"><p>
Review all your flashcards. Request additional cards for missing content or refine what you already have.
![](/imgs/review-example.png)
</p></Step>
<Step title="Export">
<p>
Export to your favorite spaced repetition app.
![](/imgs/export-example-1.png)
</p>
</Step>
</Steps>
