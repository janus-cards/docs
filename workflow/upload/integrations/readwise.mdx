## Overview
The [Readwise](https://readwise.io/) integration is one of Janus' most compelling features. Readwise is a 3rd party service that aggregates your **highlights** across multiple documents and resources. Other tools, such as [Snipd](https://www.snipd.com/), sync to this library allowing you to leverage this workflow across a broader set of media, such as podcasts and books.


![Readwise library](/imgs/readwise-library.png)

## Setting Up
 In order to sync your Readwise library to Janus, you must copy your "access token" from your readwise account to your settings. You can your token [here](https://readwise.io/access_token). We only read content from your library and never modify it.
 ![Settings section for readwise integration](/imgs/readwise-settings.png)

It may take a few minutes for your entire library to sync initially, but your most recent documents and highlights should be immediately available.
## Selecting What To Upload
You can only select one document at a time to upload by clicking the row in the Readwise document table.

### Search
Search queries scan across both the title and authors of your documents. Matches do not have to be precise to be shown.
![Example of search features with readwise integration](/imgs/rest-is-politics-example.png)
### Selecting Chapters
If the original source contains chapter outlines, you can narrow what you make flashcards to a smaller chunk of material. This keeps notebooks small, AI costs lower and also allows for a simple version of **incremental reading** where you generate cards after each chapter you read.

At the moment, only **epubs** documents are supported and must come from their premium read-it-later app [Reader](https://readwise.io/read) 

![Example of Readwise chapter selection, using Cold Start Problem as example](/imgs/readwise-chapter-selection.png)

## Types of Documents
- **Full**: The original source text is available. The notebook will be comprised of this text, and any matching highlights.
- **Highlight Only**: The original source text was not available. The notebook contains all the highlights + notes as text, one after another.
