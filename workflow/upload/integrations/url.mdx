### Overview
For knowledge workers who need to digest a lot of new online material, it's helpful to be able to **read, highlight and make flashcards in one app**. With Janus, you can import webpages, articles and essays, and then use the powerful [document reader](/workflow/notebook/document-reader) workflows to do this.
