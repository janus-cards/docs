### Overview
You can upload a plain block of text or **markdown** for free. This is useful for creating cards from notes written in **Obsidian** or **Notion**.

![Example of text input, using characters from Oppenheimer's story](/imgs/text-input.png)

#### Previewing Content
You can see how the document will be rendered by toggling the preview button.

![Preview for how text documents will be rendered](/imgs/text-input-preview.png)
