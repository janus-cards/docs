## Overview
You can upload recorded lectures, videos or audio files with Janus. These will be transcribed to text, and then further formatted with headings for ease of reading. 

We support a variety of formats including: `.mp3`, `.mp4`, and `.wav`.

![Example of Uploading a video to Janus](/imgs/janus-video.png)
### Selecting the Document
To select the the document, you can either drag and drop the file from your file explorer to the input area, or click the input area for the file explorer to pop up.
