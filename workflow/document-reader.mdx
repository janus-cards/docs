## Overview
Once your material has been uploaded, the converted text will appear in the **Document Reader**. The Document Reader allows you to view text, select sections to create new highlights, view existing highlights, and quickly generate cards for a particular highlight.

![Document Reader next to controls](/imgs/document-reader-at-a-glance.png)
## Existing Highlights
Highlights are overlayed on top of the document text, and are colour coded:
- **Blue with stripes** = Proposed highlights that are not yet saved (see [proposing highlights](/workflows/generate))
- **Yellow** = Saved highlight with no flaschards
- **Green** = Saved highlight with flashcards

![Different colored highlights](/imgs/document-reader-color-codes.png)

## Creating New Highlights
You can select a region of text, and it will be automatically turned into a saved highlight. For inline or block **maths text**, the entire equation is converted to a highlight even when only a portion is selected.

## Generating Cards
Hovering over an existing highlight will open a tooltip above. In addition to being able to quickly delete the highlight, you can run the card generator on the highlight with an optional prompt. 
![Tooltip above highlights in the document reader](/imgs/document-reader-tooltip.png)
